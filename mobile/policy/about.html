DataStrap

Your Google Health data, scored on your own device.

DataStrap is a personal dashboard. After you sign in with Google, it downloads your activity, sleep and heart-rate data and works out:

  • Recovery: a daily 0–100 score from your heart rate variability, resting heart rate, breathing rate and skin temperature, compared with your own baseline.
  • Strain: a 0–21 score for how hard you worked, from your heart rate.
  • Sleep: stages, efficiency and an estimated sleep score.
  • Workouts: heart-rate zones, peak heart rate and recovery for each session.

Your data stays with you

DataStrap runs in your browser or on your phone. Your health data is stored on your device, not on a DataStrap server. It has no advertising or analytics code.
