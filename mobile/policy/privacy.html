DataStrap Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

DataStrap is a personal health dashboard. It runs in your web browser or on your phone and works out recovery, strain, sleep and related scores from your own Fitbit data. This policy explains what happens to your information.

Summary

  • Your health data is stored on your device, not on a DataStrap server. The developer does not receive it.
  • DataStrap reads your data directly from the Google Health API after you sign in with Google.
  • DataStrap has no advertising, analytics or tracking code, and does not sell or share your data.

Data DataStrap reads from Google

When you sign in, you grant DataStrap read-only access to your Google Health data: activity and fitness, sleep, health metrics and measurements (such as heart rate, heart rate variability, SpO₂, skin temperature and breathing rate), settings, and your profile (name, age, height, weight and time zone).

DataStrap asks for one write permission, activity and fitness (write), only if you use the Log + feature to record a workout. Without it, the app cannot save workouts to your Google Health account.

Where your data is stored

Downloaded data is kept in your browser's storage (IndexedDB) or the app's private storage on your device. Your health data is never uploaded to a DataStrap server. You can remove it by clearing the site's data in your browser, or by deleting the app from your home screen.

Sign-in helper

So that the web version stays signed in for longer than an hour, it uses a small sign-in helper run by the developer on Cloudflare Workers. When you sign in, and about once an hour after that, the app sends the helper Google's one-time sign-in code or your sign-in renewal token. The helper adds the app's client secret, passes the request to Google, and returns Google's reply to your device. It never sees your health data, and it does not store or log these codes or tokens. The Android app doesn't use it.

Optional Google Takeout import

If you import a Google Takeout export, the file is read on your device. Only the values DataStrap needs, such as VO₂ max, are kept. The file itself is not uploaded.

Feedback form

If you use "Send feedback" in the app, the message you write is sent to the developer through a Google Form. A contact address is sent only if you enter one.

If a page fails to load, the app can show a "Send report" button. Pressing it sends the error text to the same form. Nothing is sent automatically.

Both feedback and error reports include a short technical note: the app version, the date and time, the platform, and the most recent error message, if any. This note does not include your health data. Don't put health details in a message unless you are happy for the developer to read them.

Google sign-out

You can revoke DataStrap's access at any time in your Google Account settings (myaccount.google.com, under Security, third-party access). DataStrap also sends a revoke request to Google when you sign out.

Use of Google user data

DataStrap's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Children

DataStrap is not intended for children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent with the "Send feedback" form in the app.
